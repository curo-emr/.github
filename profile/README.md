# Curo EMR

Open-source electronic medical records for clinics, laboratories and pharmacies.

Curo gives everyone around a patient visit (receptionists, nurses, doctors, lab staff,
pharmacists, and patients themselves) a portal of their own, on one FHIR-shaped API.

- **[curo](https://github.com/curo-emr/curo)**: the platform. NestJS services, PostgreSQL,
  and seven Next.js portals.
